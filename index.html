        How the app keeps dinner safe

        Every dish leaves out fish, eggs, dairy, tree nuts and peanuts, and has no seafood, so all six of you eat the same dinner. Isla and Beckett can add cheese on the side when a recipe says so.

        The app checks ingredients, not products. Brands change recipes and factory lines, so read every label, especially for fish and "may contain tree nuts".
